# Nimbus Labs Expense Policy

**Document owner:** Riya Sharma · **Version:** 1.1 · **Last reviewed:** 1 Oct 2026

## Limits per category
| Category | Limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local travel (cab, auto) | Rs 1,500 per ride | Receipt required, except auto-rickshaws up to Rs 200 |
| Domestic flight | Rs 8,000 one way | Economy class only |
| Train | Up to AC 2-tier fare | |
| Hotel | Rs 9,000 per night in Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru; Rs 6,000 elsewhere | GST invoice in the company name where possible |
| Meals while travelling | Breakfast Rs 500, lunch Rs 800, dinner Rs 1,500 per person | Number of covers must be on the bill |
| Team lunch (office) | Rs 800 per person | |
| Software subscriptions | Up to Rs 5,000 per month without prior approval | Above that, approval by the Engineering Manager before purchase |
| Office supplies | Rs 5,000 per purchase | |
| Client entertainment | Prior written approval from Riya Sharma | Not covered by the meal limits |

## Receipts
- Every claim needs a receipt or GST invoice showing merchant, date, GST split and total.
- The same invoice number cannot be claimed twice. A duplicate is rejected and the claimant is notified.
- Claims must be submitted within 15 days of the expense.

## Approval
- Claims up to Rs 25,000 are approved by Karan Malhotra. Riya Sharma is the final approver above Rs 25,000.
- Nobody approves their own claim. Karan's claims go to Riya.
- A claim that breaks a category limit is approved only for the allowed amount unless the approver signs off the excess in writing.

## Card use
Company cards are for business expenses only. Every card transaction must be matched to a receipt within 15 days; unmatched transactions are chased by Finance.
