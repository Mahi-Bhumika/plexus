# Engineer onboarding guide

## Day 1
1. Collect your company laptop. It comes with device management installed and disk encryption on.
2. Accounts are created from your role template. Engineers start with Slack, Google Workspace, GitHub, Figma and Jira.
3. Join the Slack channels: `#general`, `#eng-tasks`, `#it-access` and `#finance`.
4. Sign the confidentiality agreement and the acceptable use policy.

## First week
- Finish the security awareness training within 7 days. Production access stays closed until you do.
- Need another tool? Post in `#it-access`. Low-risk apps are approved automatically; others go to your manager.
- Production access is granted just-in-time, for a few hours at a time, never permanently.
- Read the reconciliation repo README and the current sprint board.
