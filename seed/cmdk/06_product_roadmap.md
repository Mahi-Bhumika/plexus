# Nimbus Ledger Sync roadmap

| Quarter | Item |
| --- | --- |
| Q4 2026 | Meridian UPI reconciliation pilot, go-live 30 Nov. Exceptions queue for unmatched transactions |
| Q1 2027 | NEFT and IMPS reconciliation (Meridian phase two). Scheduled exports to CSV and Excel |
| Q2 2027 | Reconciliation analytics dashboard. Work toward ISO 27001 certification, planned for 2027 |

## Not committed
Customer-managed encryption keys and on-premise deployment are not on the committed roadmap.

*Roadmap dates are plans, not commitments to customers.*
