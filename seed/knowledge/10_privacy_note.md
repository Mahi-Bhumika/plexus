# Nimbus Labs Privacy Note

**Document owner:** Riya Sharma · **Version:** 1.2 · **Last reviewed:** 5 Oct 2026

## Approach
Nimbus Labs designs Nimbus Ledger Sync for alignment with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. This is a design goal, not a certification or a compliance statement.

## Roles
For the transaction data it processes for banks, Nimbus Labs acts as a data processor on the customer's instructions. The customer remains the data fiduciary.

## Data location
Customer data is **stored in India by default** (AWS Mumbai). Disaster recovery copies are held in Singapore, as described in the backup plan.

## Use of data
- Nimbus Labs **does not sell** customer data.
- Customer data is not used to train machine learning models.
- Access by Nimbus Labs staff is limited to support and operations needs, under the access control policy.

## Individual requests
When a bank forwards a request from an individual (access, correction or erasure), Nimbus Labs responds to the bank **within 15 days**.

## Privacy contact
Questions go to privacy@nimbuslabs.example. Riya Sharma is the named privacy lead.

## Certifications
Nimbus Labs holds a SOC 2 Type II report. It **does not yet hold ISO 27001 certification**; certification is planned for 2027.
