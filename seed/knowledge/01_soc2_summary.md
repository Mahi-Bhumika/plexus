# Nimbus Labs SOC 2 Type II Summary

**Document owner:** Karan Malhotra, Engineering Manager · **Version:** 2.1 · **Last reviewed:** 20 Aug 2026

## Overview
Nimbus Labs completed a SOC 2 Type II examination covering the Nimbus Ledger Sync platform. The audit period ran from 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026. The final report was issued on 14 August 2026 by the independent auditor Harlow & Pike LLP.

## Trust Services Criteria in scope
Three criteria were examined:
- **Security** (common criteria)
- **Availability**
- **Confidentiality**

The Processing Integrity and Privacy criteria were not part of this examination.

## Results
The auditor noted two exceptions during the period:
1. One quarterly access review was completed 6 days after the due date in Q1 2026.
2. One terminated contractor retained read-only access to a staging tool for 2 days after their contract ended.

Both exceptions were remediated and closed before the report was issued. No exceptions related to encryption, backups or incident response.

## Scope boundaries
The report covers the production platform hosted on AWS (Mumbai primary, Singapore disaster recovery), the corporate identity provider and the ticketing system. It does not cover customer-operated systems.

## Sharing the report
The full report is available to customers and prospects under a signed NDA. Requests go to security@nimbuslabs.example and are answered within 3 business days. A bridge letter covering the period from 1 July 2026 to the date of request can be issued on demand.

## Next examination
The next Type II audit period begins 1 July 2026 and runs for 12 months.
