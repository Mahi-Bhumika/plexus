# Nimbus Labs Vendor Management Policy

**Document owner:** Riya Sharma · **Version:** 1.3 · **Last reviewed:** 3 Aug 2026

## Vendor tiers
Vendors are placed in three tiers:
- **Tier 1:** handles customer data or is critical to running the platform.
- **Tier 2:** handles internal business data only.
- **Tier 3:** no access to company or customer data.

Nimbus Labs currently has **14 Tier 1 vendors**.

## Due diligence
Before a Tier 1 vendor is onboarded, Nimbus Labs reviews its security reports (for example SOC 2 or ISO 27001), signs a data processing agreement and records the review in the vendor register. The Engineering Manager approves every Tier 1 vendor.

## Ongoing review
Tier 1 vendors are **reviewed once a year**. Tier 2 vendors are reviewed every two years. Any vendor that suffers a security incident is reviewed immediately.

## Sub-processors
Nimbus Labs uses **9 sub-processors** that may process customer data, including AWS and an email delivery provider. The current list is shared with customers on request.

## Change notice
Customers receive **30 days' advance notice** before a new sub-processor is added. A customer may object in writing within that period.

## Exit
When a vendor is removed, Nimbus Labs confirms in writing that data held by the vendor has been returned or deleted.
