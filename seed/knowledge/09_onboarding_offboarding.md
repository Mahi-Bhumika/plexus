# Nimbus Labs Onboarding and Offboarding Procedure

**Document owner:** Riya Sharma · **Version:** 1.5 · **Last reviewed:** 22 Sep 2026

## Before the first day
A **background check** (identity, employment history and education) is completed **before the start date** for every employee, intern and contractor.

## Onboarding
- A company laptop is issued with **mobile device management (MDM)** installed and disk encryption enforced.
- Accounts are created from the person's role template; additional access is requested through the access request process.
- **Security awareness training** must be completed **within 7 days of joining**. Access to production systems is not available until it is complete.
- Everyone signs a confidentiality agreement and the acceptable use policy.
- Annual refresher training is required for all staff.

## Offboarding
- The manager notifies HR on or before the last working day.
- **All access is revoked within 4 hours** of the person's exit time.
- Shared secrets that the person had access to are rotated within 2 business days.
- The company laptop is returned, and the device is **wiped within 3 business days**.
- Offboarding is recorded in the audit log and checked in the next quarterly access review.

## Contractors and interns
Contractors and interns follow the same procedure. Their access ends automatically on the contract end date unless extended in writing.
